# RapidBookr

> Tourism booking & transfer management SaaS — by RapidBizz.

One operations platform for tourism and travel operators: bookings, suppliers, drivers, staff and clients — with a separate portal for each role.

## What it does

- **Admin portal** — the control centre: bookings, operations and reporting
- **Staff portal** — day-to-day operations for the team
- **Supplier / Driver portal** — coordination with suppliers and drivers
- **Client portal** — client-facing booking flow
- Role-based access: each user is directed to their own portal

## Who it's for

Tourism and travel operators in the UAE and beyond who still manage bookings manually — spreadsheets, WhatsApp and phone calls.

## Status

Own product of RapidBizz Pvt. Ltd. — in development.

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase.
